Data Processed: When you generate recipes, PantryChef transmits your entered pantry ingredients, dietary preference, cuisine selection, and serving count to OpenRouter AI models to synthesize recipe suggestions.

Local Storage & Your Rights: Account credentials (hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) and saved favorite recipes are stored in your local SQLite database.
